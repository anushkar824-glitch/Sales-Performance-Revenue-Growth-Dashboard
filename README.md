# Sales Performance & Revenue Growth Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project uses Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, revenue growth, profitability, regional performance, product categories, and sales target achievement.

## Objectives
- Monitor total revenue, profit, and units sold.
- Compare monthly sales targets with actual sales.
- Analyze revenue across regions and product categories.
- Compare Online and Offline sales channels.
- Evaluate sales executive performance.
- Track achievement rate and average growth rate.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- Microsoft Excel
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)

## Dataset
The dataset contains sales information, including dates, regions, sales executives, product categories, units sold, revenue, cost, profit, sales targets, actual sales, and growth percentages.

The dataset is synthetic and created for learning and portfolio demonstration.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Sales Overview
- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Total Units Sold
- Achievement Rate
- Average Growth Rate
- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Revenue by Region
- Monthly Target vs Actual Sales
- Region, Month, and Sales Channel filters

### 2. Product & Performance Analysis
- Revenue by Product Category
- Revenue by Sales Channel
- Revenue by Sales Executive
- Product Category and Sales Executive filters

## Key Skills Demonstrated
- Data cleaning and preparation
- Data modeling and DAX measures
- KPI development
- Interactive dashboard design
- Sales and profitability analysis
- Business performance reporting

## How to Use
1. Download the `.pbix` file and Excel dataset.
2. Open the Power BI file using Power BI Desktop.
3. Use the slicers to explore sales performance across different categories.

## Project Purpose
This project was developed for learning and portfolio purposes to demonstrate practical Power BI and business analytics skills.
